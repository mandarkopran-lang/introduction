03 • History

Our Journey of Evolution

1958
Incorporated as Kopran Chemical Company Pvt. Ltd. on April 26.
1984 – 1985
Converted to Public Limited; Technical cooperation with Yuhan Corp (South Korea) for Cephalexin & Rifampicin.
1990 – 1992
Rebranded as Kopran Limited; Launched Omeprazole (LOKIT); Successful equity public issue.
1996 – 2001
Won Trishul Export Award; Strategic alliances with Synpac (UK) and E. Merck Ltd; Patent approval for NCE KNC-6.
2013 – 2018
Launched SPARKLE FMCG brand; Acquired state-of-the-art API manufacturing facility at Panoli, Gujarat.
Present
Continuous technological innovation, cGMP compliance, and global regulatory expansions.

14 • Operations

Quality Manufacturing & cGMP Compliance

Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP)

  • Rigorous adherence to approved Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
  • Mandatory gowning and personal protective equipment (PPE) protocols.
  • Strict plant uniform color codes across distinct processing zones.
  • Preventive maintenance and automated HVAC environmental controls.

Cleanrooms & Cross-Contamination Prevention

Our facilities utilize controlled cleanroom areas, differential pressure systems, and strict entry airlocks to completely eliminate cross-contamination hazards between products.

  • HEPA filtration and environmental parameter monitoring.
  • Differential pressure gradients to isolate processing zones.
  • Dedicated airlock systems for material and personnel entry.
